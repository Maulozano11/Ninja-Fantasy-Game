Chakra type review fixes: the clan no longer changes an old ninja's nature

- Changing clan in the creator no longer writes the clan's colour into
  palette.chakraAuraColor. A sheet older than the sprite look draws its
  burst from that colour, so a clan change switched its chakra type (a
  Northstar hero went from Agua to Fuego or Viento). The type is now only
  the player's choice in Appearance > Chakra.
- The pose bar stays between the side panels and wraps when there is no
  room. At 1600x900 it covered the end of the burst description (and,
  before that, the colour row); the fix holds for every section.
- A burst id the game does not know is described as Rising Tide, as it is
  drawn and as its nature reads (chakraChoice in chakraInfo.ts), not as
  the first burst (Pyre Crown). Test added.
- The creator says the chakra type will soon decide the elemental jutsu:
  the gating is not built yet.
- GAMEPLAY_DESIGN "Chakra type": only the owner's rule is marked decided;
  the burst-to-type table, the Earth burst, the four types without an
  element and the legends' new types are marked as proposals waiting for
  his approval. Saved legends and companions keep their old burst: added
  to Open questions and STATUS as a decision for him.
