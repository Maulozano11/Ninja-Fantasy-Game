  still has its old burst loads with the legend's burst of today, in the

- Shadow and Spirit are pending: out of the creator's type tiles and the
  random ninja (CHAKRA_TYPES `pending`, OFFERED_TYPES). A ninja that has one
  keeps it, draws it and reads it; the creator shows its tile marked
  "Pendiente" and lets the player switch away. Light and Void stay; a hint
  says their own jutsu come later.
- The nature gates the elemental jutsu (src/rules/jutsuNature.ts): the
  Element I, Element II and S-rank slots offer only jutsu of the ninja's
  element plus the ones with no element; Utility and the clan secret are
  not gated; Light and Void get no elemental jutsu yet. Changing the type
  swaps the jutsu of another element (own element, lowest rank first) and a
  note says which. A sheet's own off-nature jutsu (Kaien's Fireball, Raiga's
  Earth Wall, older sheets) stay, marked "no es de tu naturaleza". The
  random ninja picks jutsu of its nature and its clan's secret. Enemies and
  battles are untouched; the rank cap for a new ninja is not built (his call).
