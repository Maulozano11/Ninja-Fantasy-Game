# Character poses brief: walk, run, crouch, sneak and downed

You are a senior pixel-art animator on "Shinobi 5e Chronicles", a commercial ninja tactics RPG (React + TypeScript). Characters are layered 16-bit sprites drawn by code: a skeleton rig with keyframed poses (src/sprite/rig.ts), body and equipment layers composed in src/sprite/compose.ts (body.ts, grip.ts, rotsprite.ts, items/...). Repo: /home/user/Ninja-Fantasy-Game. Read docs/SPRITE_STYLE_GUIDE.md first, then rig.ts (PoseFrame, POSES, buildRig, frameCount, POSE_FPS) and compose.ts (how poses drive layers, e.g. the handsigns pose stows the weapon).

The new battle engine moves characters freely across a big side-view map, so they need movement poses. The names are fixed and already declared (src/sprite/types.ts: MovePose = 'walk' | 'run' | 'crouch' | 'sneak' | 'downed'; rig.ts has placeholder POSES entries that just repeat IDLE, and POSE_FPS entries: walk 10, run 12, crouch 3, sneak 8, downed 2). - run: 6 frames, forward lean about 8-12 degrees, long stride with a flight phase (both feet off the ground in 1-2 frames: you may need a new PoseFrame field such as lift so the pelvis can rise above the ground solve), arms pumping with bent elbows, hair/cape/scarf trailing if the layer system allows. Seamless loop. A ninja run (Naruto style arms back) is welcome as long as it reads well; choose what looks best.
- crouch: 2 frames (breathing), low stance with knees bent and the torso leaning forward, hands ready: this is how a hidden ninja looks behind cover.
- sneak: 6 frames, a crouched walk.
- downed: lying on the ground, knocked out (2-4 frames, very slow breathing).
